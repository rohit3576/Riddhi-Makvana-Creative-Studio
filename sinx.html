BRAND STRATEGY &
DESIGN

Brand & Client Understanding

Snitch targets Gen Z with trendy, affordable fashion; the client aims to expand into eco-friendly, stylish, and customizable sneakers.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Young consumers lack sneakers that combine affordability, style, durability, and sustainability, as current options are either expensive or low-quality.

OBJECTIVES

Marketing: Expand into sneakers, grow visibility, and stand out through customization.
Sales: Drive launch sales, increase revenue, boost conversions, and repeat purchases.
Communication: Promote self-expression, highlight comfort & quality, and build hype with consistent messaging.

STP STRATEGY

SEGMENTATION

Gen Z (18-25), urban youth, streetwear-focused, trend-driven & social media active.

TARGETING

Niche focus on Gen Z seeking affordable, stylish, luxury-inspired sneakers.

POSITIONING

S!NX as a youth-centric, affordable premium sneaker brand promoting bold self-expression.

INTRODUCING

THE ORIGINALS

Sustainable sneakers made by upcycling Snitch fabric waste into unique, limited-edition styles.

GLOW

Color-changing sneakers using UV-reactive pigments for a dynamic look.

SWITCH

Modular sneakers with detachable recycled panels for customization.

Materials: Organic cotton, recycled mesh & rubber, bio-based EVA, recycled laces, eco-friendly adhesives.

CAMPAIGN CONCEPT

Awareness: Teasers, OOH & campus activations to spark curiosity.

Hype: Influencers & storytelling around customization.

Experience: Try-ons, demos & exclusive launch offers.

Stunt: Interactive installation for engagement & shareability.

Retention: UGC, referrals & community building.

IMC STRATEGY

PRE-LAUNCH

Teasers, influencers, campus buzz, OOH & early access – Build hype & waitlist

→

LAUNCH

Ads, influencer reveals, events, pop-ups & offers – Drive reach, trials & sales

→

POST-LAUNCH

UGC, referrals, retargeting & community building – Boost loyalty & repeat purchase

PACKAGING

PACKAGING CONCEPT:
THE S!NX BOX IS DESIGNED AS A REUSABLE PACKAGING THAT CONVERTS INTO A SNEAKER DISPLAY STAND, COMBINING SUSTAINABILITY WITH A STYLISH UNBOXING AND PRODUCT SHOWCASE EXPERIENCE.
