CEAT Limited is one of India's leading tyre manufacturers, specializing in high-performance tyres and automotive accessories for passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, and commercial vehicles, known for its innovation, safety standards, and strong global presence.

CEAT's product portfolio is primarily focused on tyres; however, the brand has strong potential to expand into a sub-brand specializing in automotive accessories such as premium seat covers and steering covers. This extension would leverage CEAT's existing trust in performance and safety while positioning the brand for future growth in comfort, design, and in-car experience solutions.

Conceptualized a brand extension for CEAT - ASTRIS, focused on sustainable and budget-friendly automotive accessories, including seat covers and steering covers, aimed at enhancing in-car comfort while aligning with eco-conscious consumer needs.

PRODUCT RANGE

ASTRIS offers functional and stylish automotive accessories tailored to different user needs.

  • Pet-Proof Seat Covers: Scratch-resistant, easy-to-clean covers with comfort padding—ideal for pet owners, keeping seats protected and mess-free.
  • Luxury Seat Covers: Premium quilted and matte-finish covers with added foam, lumbar support, and headrest for a refined, comfortable experience.
  • Sports Seat Covers: Bold tyre-tread design with high-grip texture, built for a sporty look and better seating stability.
  • Rough & Tough Steering Cover: Durable, high-grip cover designed for enhanced control and long-lasting use.

Strategically positioned ASTRIS to capture an untapped market segment, leveraging CEAT's established brand loyalty to drive adoption and gain first-mover advantage in automotive accessories.
